# Campaign readiness checklist

Before enabling live outreach, require all of the following for every lead selected for launch:

- Current company and role are confirmed from a reliable source (prefer the lead's LinkedIn profile).
- The person matches the Altisec ICP: senior security, IT, technology, risk, or relevant transformation leader at a sufficiently sized company.
- The email/phone belongs to the same current employer or is explicitly retained as a personal/professional contact method.
- Duplicate LinkedIn URLs, emails, and phones are resolved.
- The lead's location and company priority are considered; Bengaluru/India targets can be ranked ahead of non-priority regions.
- Research card and copy reference the corrected role/company and contain no legacy Urbanebolt/logistics positioning.
- Channel sequence is deduplicated: one coordinated plan across email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp, with reply/meeting suppression enabled.
- Reply monitoring is verified before live email sending.
- Dry-run and one internal test pass before external launch.

Do not interpret a saved draft as a verified or approved lead. The campaign should launch only from the verified cohort, with unresolved records held for review.
